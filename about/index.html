关于我

“一个喜欢用代码解决生活问题的人。”

Hi, I'm Tianchen. I live and work in the Bay Area, California. I'm passionate about programming and photography — two crafts that let me both build and capture the world around me.

When I'm not writing code, you'll find me exploring America's national parks, watching anime and films that tell compelling stories, or diving into books that challenge my thinking.

This site is my personal space — a curated collection of the things I love and the experiences that have shaped who I am. It's part travel journal, part media library, and part reading list.
